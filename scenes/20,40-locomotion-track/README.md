# Locomotion Track (20,40)

A flat, open track for measuring player locomotion (walk, jog, run, stops, direction changes) frame by frame and
comparing explorers against each other.

## Layout

- **Parcels** 20,40 to 29,47 (10 x 8). The open track is 20,40 to 27,47: 128 m x 128 m with no colliders. The
  side area 28,40 to 29,47 holds a 48 m wall for collision tests, east of an orange line that marks the track edge.
- **Spawn**: one default spawn point at the exact scene position `(64, 0, 64)`, the track centre (world position
  `(384, 0, 704)`, parcel 24,44), looking north. Every direction has 64 m of open ground, the diagonals about 90 m.
- **Grid**: thin lines every 1 m, stronger every 5 m, white every 10 m, aligned so a 10 m line runs through the spawn.
  Blue lines mark parcel borders; the north-south axis through the spawn is blue, the east-west axis red.
- **Labels**: distance from the spawn every 10 m along each axis (`N 10 m` ... `W 60 m`), the parcel coordinates at
  every parcel centre, and large N/E/S/W letters at the track edges.
- The scene adds no `AvatarModifierArea`, `InputModifier` or `AvatarLocomotionSettings`, so each explorer moves the
  player at its default speeds.

## Readout

A small panel shows the player's scene position, offset from the spawn, horizontal speed (averaged over 0.1 s),
peak speed, heading (0 = north, 90 = east) and distance moved. It updates at most 10 times a second.

- `1`: show or hide the panel (sampling stops while hidden).
- `2`: reset peak speed and distance.

## Running

Locally: `npm install`, then `npm run start` (pnpm: `pnpm install`, `pnpm start`).

Deployed: the scene is in the `sdk7testscenes.dcl.eth` world on the zone environment. Arrive with
`?position=24,44`, for example `decentraland://?dclenv=zone&realm=sdk7testscenes.dcl.eth&position=24,44`; any parcel
of the scene leads to the spawn point.

The grid texture `assets/grid-10m.png` is generated by `npm run grid-texture` (pnpm: `pnpm run grid-texture`).
