# Locomotion Track (20,40)

A flat, open track and a geometry course for measuring player locomotion (walk, jog, run, stops, direction changes,
jumps, ledges, stairs, slopes, walls) frame by frame and comparing explorers against each other.

## Layout

- **Parcels** 20,40 to 29,47 (10 x 8). The open track is 20,40 to 27,47: 128 m x 128 m with no colliders. The
  geometry course is 28,40 to 29,47 (scene x 128 to 160), east of an orange line that marks the track edge.
- **Spawn**: one default spawn point at the exact scene position `(64, 0, 64)`, the track centre (world position
  `(384, 0, 704)`, parcel 24,44), looking north. Every direction has 64 m of open ground, the diagonals about 90 m.
- **Grid**: thin lines every 1 m, stronger every 5 m, white every 10 m, aligned so a 10 m line runs through the spawn.
  Blue lines mark parcel borders; the north-south axis through the spawn is blue, the east-west axis red.
- **Labels**: distance from the spawn every 10 m along each axis (`N 10 m` ... `W 60 m`), the parcel coordinates at
  every parcel centre, and large N/E/S/W letters at the track edges.
- The scene adds no `AvatarModifierArea`, `InputModifier` or `AvatarLocomotionSettings`, so each explorer moves the
  player at its default speeds.

## Geometry course

Every feature starts at scene x = 130, 2 m east of the track edge, and faces the track. All of it is primitive boxes
with box colliders, so every explorer collides with the same shapes. Each item has a ground label at x = 129. The
numbers are in `src/layout.ts`.

Stairs and slopes are coloured against Unity Explorer's defaults (step offset 0.35 m, slope limit 46 degrees): green
below, yellow equal, red above.

| Feature | Scene position | Sizes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ledges | x 130..140, 4 m wide, z 2, 8, 14, 20, 26, 32 (south to north) | tops 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4.7 and 10 m; vertical west edge at x = 130 |
| Stairs | from x = 130 rising east, 3 m wide, z 40, 44 ... 64 | risers 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.35, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6 m; 6 steps, 0.5 m tread, 2 m top step |
| Slopes | rising east to a plateau 3 m high at x 148..152, 2.4 m wide, z 70 to 100.4 every 2.8 m | 10, 20, 30, 40, 45, 46, 47, 50, 60, 70, 80 degrees |
| Wall | x 150..150.5, z 103..127 | 3 m high, 24 m long |

- **Ledges**: walking west off a ledge lands on the open track, so the landing distance can be read off its grid. A
  yellow line marks the edge and a white line on the top marks 1 m back from it.
- **Slopes**: a ramp's bottom is 3 / tan(angle) m west of the plateau (the 10 degree ramp starts at x = 131). Stand on
  the plateau and walk west to step onto a slope from the top.
- Named spawn points (not default) put the player at a feature:

| Spawn point | Position | Facing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ledge-0.5`, `ledge-1`, `ledge-2`, `ledge-3`, `ledge-4.7`, `ledge-10` | `(131, height, 4 / 10 / 16 / 22 / 28 / 34)`: on the ledge, 1 m from its edge | west |
| `stairs` | `(129, 0, 53.5)`: before the 0.35 m stairs | east |
| `slopes` | `(129, 0, 85.2)`: before the 46 degree ramp | east |
| `slopes-top` | `(150, 3, 85.2)`: on the plateau above the 46 degree ramp | west |
| `wall` | `(138, 0, 115)` | east |

## Readout

A small panel shows the player's scene position, offset from the spawn, horizontal speed (averaged over 0.1 s),
peak speed, heading (0 = north, 90 = east) and distance moved. It updates at most 10 times a second.

- `1`: show or hide the panel (sampling stops while hidden).
- `2`: reset peak speed and distance.

## Running

Locally: `npm install`, then `npm run start` (pnpm: `pnpm install`, `pnpm start`).

Deployed: the scene is in the `sdk7testscenes.dcl.eth` world on the zone environment. Arrive with
`?position=24,44`, for example `decentraland://?dclenv=zone&realm=sdk7testscenes.dcl.eth&position=24,44`; any parcel
of the scene leads to the default spawn point. Inside the world, the chat command
`/goto sdk7testscenes.dcl.eth/24,44/ledge-4.7` lands on a named spawn point; a `/goto` without the world name leaves
the world for Genesis City.

The grid texture `assets/grid-10m.png` is generated by `npm run grid-texture` (pnpm: `pnpm run grid-texture`).
