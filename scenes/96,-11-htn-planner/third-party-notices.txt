# Third-party notices: canonical HTN examples

The domains under `src/examples/canon` are ports of published HTN examples. Where a domain is translated or adapted
from permissively licensed code, that code's copyright line and licence text are below; each domain's header says
what was taken and what changed. Sources re-expressed from published descriptions (book chapters, slides and
papers) are credited in the domain headers and below. Preserve the credits together with the notices when
distributing these examples.

One section per source, grouped by the example set that uses it.

## Bridge troll (`bridge_troll/`) and ore to gold (`crafting/`)

### bevy_htn

- Source: https://github.com/RJ/bevy_htn, files `bevy_htn/assets/troll.htn` and `bevy_htn/assets/miner.htn`, commit
  `422c8614ec1cc4d7f0382d93a835bcc6eab36d80`.
- Used in: `bridge_troll/v3_rearm.domain`, `bridge_troll/v4_last_seen.domain` (and the steps after them, which keep
  their methods). Adapted: renamed, re-shaped for a planner without effect simulation (deferred `&` tasks instead of
  effects and expected effects), host sensors instead of the find operators. bevy_htn's troll is itself based on
  Troy Humphreys' chapter (Game AI Pro, ch. 12), which the series re-expresses and credits.
- Also used in: `crafting/ore_to_gold.domain` (from `miner.htn`: the EarnGold method order, the smelt and sell
  sub-tasks, the goal of 3 gold). Adapted: deferred self-calls instead of simulated effects, guards on the methods
  the source leaves unguarded, one `!go_to` for the GoTo* primitives, unused primitives left out.
- Licence: dual MIT OR Apache-2.0 (`Cargo.toml`: `license = "MIT OR Apache-2.0"`, author Richard Jones); used here

## swarm_drone, swarm_coordinator (`swarm/`)

Credits (re-expressed):
- Tim Verweij, "HTN planning in DECIMA", AI and Games Conference 2024 (slides and speaker notes,
  https://www.guerrilla-games.com/read/htn-planning-in-decima), and its 2026 AI and Games Summer School version
  ("From Byrd Box to Debug Boxes: HTN Introduction and Application in DECIMA"): the swarm member's `behave`,
  `do_idle_behavior` with continue branches, `move_to_grab_resource` with `!begin_plan` / `!end_plan`, roles as
  facts from a group agent, the "all swarm followers nearby" axiom.
- David Speck, "Building the AI behaviors for flying and swimming machines for Horizon Forbidden West" (2024): the
  intercept / attack / evade loop behind the placeholder combat.

## villager_day (`villager_day/`)

Credits (re-expressed): John-Paul Kelly, Adi Botea, Sven Koenig, "Offline Planning with Hierarchical Task Networks
in Video Games", Proceedings of AIIDE 2008 (AAAI), https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AIIDE/article/download/18673/18450 :
spending a game hour by four ordered methods with a random last one, obtaining food by four alternatives, needs and
shops with their keepers in the world state.

## arrest_team (`arrest_team/`)

Credits (re-expressed): Peter Gorniak, Ian Davis, "SquadSmart: Hierarchical Planning and Coordinated Plan Execution
for Squads of Characters", Proceedings of AIIDE 2007 (AAAI),
https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AIIDE/article/download/18775/18551 : the autonomous restrain (closest member, restrain
with a covering complement or alone, reservations, a barrier, freeing), the squad domain's shape and its execution
semantics (busy members, actions in parallel, internal operators run at once).
